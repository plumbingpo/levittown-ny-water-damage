Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. When it happens in Levittown, Levittown Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Sewage Cleanup FAQ

Emergency sewage removal and decontamination typically takes 1 to 3 days to complete. Complete restoration, including material replacement, may take 1 to 3 weeks depending on the extent of damage. Our first priority is making the property safe to occupy again as fast as we can.
What each item is made of decides that. Hard, non-porous items such as metal, glass, sealed tile and solid plastic can usually be cleaned and disinfected. Anything porous the sewage touched must go, which covers mattresses, upholstered furniture, drywall, insulation, carpet and padding. We document what is removed so you have a record for your insurance claim.
Temporary relocation is what we usually recommend, particularly for a backup that contaminated a large part of the home. Airborne pathogens and the chemicals used for decontamination can both pose health risks. We will advise you based on the specific situation in your home.
A blocked sewer line on the property is the most common starting point. Typical culprits are tree roots entering the pipe, grease hardening inside it and wipes or similar items flushed down a toilet. Sometimes an aging pipe cracks or caves in, or a storm overwhelms the city sewer and forces sewage up the floor drains. A camera inspection after cleanup shows which one you are dealing with.
Typical pricing for this work in Levittown is $2,000 to $10,000 and up, with the size of the affected area and the degree of contamination setting the number. A small backup that stayed contained might be $1,500 to $3,000, but widespread sewage flooding can pass $15,000 once replacement materials and reconstruction are counted.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
